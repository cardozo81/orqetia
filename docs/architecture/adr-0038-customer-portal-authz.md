# ADR — Customer human identities and Client Portal memberships\n\n**Status:** Accepted  \n**Date:** 2026-10-06  \n**Issue:** #142\n\n## Decision\n\nClient Portal authentication reuses the external OIDC/MFA boundary from #54. ORQETIA\nstores no customer human password. Local authorization is based on the stable external\nidentity key `issuer + subject` plus explicit tenant/client memberships.\n\nIdentity and membership are separate records. One human identity may hold multiple\nmemberships without duplicating the OIDC subject.\n\n## Membership authority\n\nA browser-selected tenant/client is only a selection hint. The server resolves the\nauthenticated external identity and requires an exact ACTIVE membership for that\ntenant/client. It then revalidates the owner through #136 on every portal\nauthentication/resolution.\n\nConsequences:\n\n- a valid client_id outside the identity's memberships is denied;\n- cross-tenant combinations are denied;\n- disabling tenant/client invalidates access without rewriting memberships;\n- disabling the identity or membership invalidates access even when the IdP succeeds.\n\n## Roles and permissions\n\nCustomer roles are OWNER, DEVELOPER, ANALYST and VIEWER. They expand to a versioned\nserver-side permission matrix for portal, credentials, sessions/tasks, usage, estimates,\nproviders, own activity and documentation.\n\nNo customer role contains Backoffice permissions. Provider/admin/financial surfaces are\nnot represented in this matrix.\n\n## Step-up\n\n`credentials:write` requires recent MFA/step-up (baseline five minutes), matching #54\nand #24. Read-only portal navigation does not require continuous reauthentication.\n\nAuthentication-strength facts come from the trusted server-created OIDC context and\ncannot be supplied by a browser header/body.\n\n## Persistence and audit\n\nExternal identity is unique by issuer/subject. Membership is unique by\nidentity/tenant/client. Identity and membership updates use optimistic versions.\n\nCreate/status/role operations emit safe audit facts. No OAuth token, password, client\ncredential secret, provider secret or financial data belongs to this boundary.\n\n## Validation\n\nThe isolated gate covers migration, uniqueness, multi-membership, cross-tenant denial,\ndisabled identity/membership/owner, owner revalidation, permission mapping, MFA freshness\nand optimistic conflicts. No real IdP/provider is contacted.\n
